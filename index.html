The problem

Everyone's in a hurry. Almost no one arrives.

Look around online and it's the same noise everywhere: a new tactic, a new shortcut, a new app that supposedly changes everything by Tuesday. People jump from one to the next, sure that the next one is finally the one that works.

Meanwhile, a small number of people just keep doing the same unglamorous things, in the same order, for long enough that it actually adds up. They're not smarter. They're not luckier. They just stopped rushing.

There's an old story in the book about a hand pump at a well, and a thirsty traveler who nearly walked away six pumps short of water. It's a small story. It's also more or less the whole idea.
